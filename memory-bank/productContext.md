# Product Context

## Problem Statement
How to effectively present both SRE expertise and personal interests in a cohesive, engaging portfolio website that maintains a clean, minimalistic design.

## User Experience Goals
- Intuitive navigation between professional and personal sections
- Clear information hierarchy that prioritizes most relevant content
- Engaging presentation of projects, skills, and interests
- Fast-loading pages with minimal distractions
- Accessible to all users regardless of device or abilities
- Tabbed interface for personal interests to maintain clean organization

## Content Organization
- Dual-sided approach with distinct professional and personal areas
- Professional side:
  - Resume highlights (SRE experience, skills, education)
  - Technical projects (Employee Access Management Tool, UPI stack, etc.)
  - Skills visualization (AWS, Kubernetes, Go, Python, etc.)
  - Technical blog posts and case studies
- Personal side (using tabbed interface):
  - Puzzles tab (Rubik's collection, solving techniques)
  - Biking tab (Hero Maverick 440, riding experiences)
  - Trekking tab (favorite locations, experiences)
  - Music tab (IEM collection, listening preferences)
- Shared elements: About, contact information

## Key Differentiators
- Integration of the distinctive green "BG" logo throughout the site
- Balance of technical SRE content with personal interests
- Tabbed interface for clean organization of diverse interests
- Focus on problem-solving as a theme across both professional work and personal interests
